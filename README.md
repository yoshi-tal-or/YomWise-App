# YomWise

**The Hebrew date, always in front of you.**

A simple, no-nonsense Android app that puts today's Hebrew date in your
status bar and on your home screen, along with the parsha, Daf Yomi,
candle lighting times and more.

**Free. No ads. No tracking. No accounts. Always.**

If you paid for this app, you were scammed. Please ask for your money back.

## What it does

- **Status bar date:** today's Hebrew day in letters, with the month
  underneath, visible at a glance. Pull down the shade for the full date,
  parsha, upcoming chag, Daf Yomi, candle lighting or Havdalah, and the
  next zman.
- **Home screen widgets:** four sizes (4×1, 2×2, 3×2 and 4×2), all
  resizable. The text scales to fill whatever size you give them.
- **Configurable:** choose what shows up: parsha, upcoming chag, Daf Yomi,
  candle lighting / Havdalah, next zman, and optionally the Hebrew year.
- **Your location, your rules:**
  - Pick from a built-in city list, use GPS, or type coordinates.
  - Israel or diaspora holiday rules.
  - Candle lighting minutes before sunset (18, 20, 22, 30 or 40).
  - Set your elevation for accurate sunrise times.
  - The Hebrew date can change at sunset, nightfall or midnight.
- **Hebrew or English** display.
- **Works offline:** everything is calculated on your phone.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

## Privacy

- No ads, analytics or tracking of any kind.
- Your location stays on your phone.
- The only network request is the optional "Look up elevation online"
  button, which sends your coordinates once to open-meteo.com when you tap it.

## Installing

1. Download the latest APK from the **Actions** tab (open the newest
   successful run and download the artifact at the bottom).
2. Unzip it, open the APK on your phone, and allow installation from your
   browser or files app when asked.
3. Open YomWise, allow notifications, choose your location, then add a
   widget from your home screen's widget picker.

## Please note

Times are calculated, not looked up. Before relying on candle lighting or
other zmanim, compare them with your local luach or a trusted source.
Customs differ between communities, and suggestions for what should be
adjustable are welcome.
